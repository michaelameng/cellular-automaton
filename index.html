Cellular Automaton

Click cells to turn them on, then set the rules that drive the simulation. Birth (B) picks how many of a cell's eight neighbors must be on for it to switch on, and Survival (S) picks how many must be on for it to stay on. Choose several counts in either category and they combine with a logical OR.

Mutation adds a dash of chance: after each step, every cell flips on or off with the probability you set, so 0% follows the rules exactly and higher values keep the board restless. And you can change the board size just below. The buttons just above the grid let you step through generations, play or pause the simulation, drop in a random board, and reset to a blank grid, respectively.
